# Ceremonial Technology Guidelines

Ceremonial Technology is any technical system that:

- Holds, reflects, or amplifies relationships.
- Is intentionally placed inside or alongside ceremony and community
  protocols.

Guidelines:

- Always model relationships explicitly (people–land–markets–code).
- Make space in UX and flows for pauses, refusals, and “I need to ask my
  people / my Elders”.
- Persist stories and accountability logs, not just metrics.
- Allow mino-bimaadizi-daa and Mino-Miigwewin modules to inform decisions
  about what is suggested or automated.
