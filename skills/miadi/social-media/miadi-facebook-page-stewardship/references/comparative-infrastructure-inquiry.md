# Comparative infrastructure inquiry → community field note

Tracked example: `miadisabelle/kherix-hermeneia#70`. The current case workspace is `/srv/miadi/episodes/miadi-chronicle/_staging_for_new_episodes/miadi-event-broker-inquiry-hub/`. This reference is a reusable method, not a claim that Kafka, Cloudflare Queues, Hookdeck, or NATS has been selected for Miadi, nor permission to publish.

## An early intent post is allowed

Before any candidate videos or reviews exist, a draft may invite people to help shape the inquiry. Its evidence is the existing intention, episode 547/review v3, and the verified state of the relevant packages and staging notes—not claims about an unreviewed vendor demonstration. Name Kafka, Cloudflare Queues, Hookdeck, and possible NATS as paths to investigate, not equivalent finalists. Say that no broker or shared trading infrastructure has been chosen and that a new Chronicle episode is only a possibility. Ask which *event, failure, and receipt* people would want to inspect; do not ask them to vote on a vendor before orientation. Mark the draft `NOT PUBLISHED` and obtain approval of its exact final form before an outward write.

## Prepare the evidence before asking for a choice

1. Name the desired event handoff and its human-verifiable outcome. Inspect the actual producing/consuming code and current deployment state; a package name or a neighboring prototype is not evidence of an integrated Miadi event broker.
2. Commission a source-video scout for each *candidate role*: Kafka, Cloudflare Queues (Wrangler is a management tool), Hookdeck, and—if its role is confirmed in the swarm inquiry—NATS. Ask for primary or maintainer-led demonstrations with direct URLs, dates, timestamps, version/topology, what was demonstrated, and what was not. Do not invent a suitable video to fill a slot. Distinguish an event log, managed queue, and webhook delivery surface before comparing products.
3. After human selection, make at least one version-pinned Miadi Review for each of the three named initial paths; NATS can become a fourth review if a suitable source is selected. A staged review is not a published review. Give each review a specific Miadi Intent and separate source claims from proposed use.
4. Before comparing brokers, ask whether the smallest durable event path and a human-visible inbox can carry a first Miadi-native and external event with source, authority, privacy, idempotency, and replay legible. A broker may only become necessary for fanout, retention, or independent consumers; a planned provider-neutral envelope is not proof it exists. If a candidate experiment is later authorized, carry the same *named Miadi event and receipt question* through each relevant role, recording producer/consumer behavior, failure and replay, traceability, operational constraints, and data boundaries. Do not assume all four candidates solve an identical problem. Sharing a broker with a trading experiment is a separate tenancy/ownership decision.
5. Draft a public **field note** only from verified material: what the source showed, what Miadi tried, what a person could inspect, what remains open, and an `advise` question. Episode 547 and its pinned review v3 model the distinction between an exercised path and a judgment of quality; they are not proof of a deployed event bus.
6. Show Guillaume the exact Page, text, links, media, audience, authorship, AI label, and participation mode before publication. Verify public links from outside private network access; do not place an unverified internal URL in Page copy. Use a supported Meta API or human-operated Page path under the applicable authority; do not promise automated browser collection or publication simply because a logged-in session exists.
7. Preserve responses as advice, with dissent and source attribution/consent boundaries. The human who opened the inquiry may also be their own first audience and choose to comment from their personal identity; a suggested comment is not permission for the Page or agent to speak as them. Return to participants with what was heard, what evidence changed, who made the decision, and what remains unresolved. No raw comment tally or agent synthesis is community consent.

## Minimal comparison record

For each candidate, record: role in the proposed handoff; source URL and timestamps; review ID/version after publication; reproducible experiment and receipt (if any); failure/replay and security boundary; open question; community advice and its status. Use `owner/repo#issue` for tracker references. The `.agents` skill-source repository and the `.hermes` runtime-symlink repository may differ; never infer an issue's repository from the location of the symlink or use a bare `#N` across them.
